        How to Play

        1. Secret picks

        Each round, every player secretly chooses exactly 2 dry cells on the 7×7 island. All picks are revealed together.

        2. Unique wins

        A cell chosen by exactly one player is claimed — place your stone. Outer ring cells are worth 1, then 2, 3, and the center is worth 5.

        3. Collisions & whirlpools

        If two or more players pick the same cell, it becomes a deadly whirlpool. Nobody claims it — and it sweeps away stones in the four adjacent cells.

        4. The rising tide

        In rounds 3, 5 and 7 the tide floods rings from the outside in. Stones on flooded land are banked for points. You'll get a warning the round before.

        5. Endgame

        After 8 rounds, remaining stones are banked. Highest score wins. Ties break by most banked stones; still tied means a shared victory.
